# Three-motor bench assembly review

The corrected parts pass the checks required for a detached, unpowered motor
assembly. The [release report](release-report.json) contains the exact STEP hashes,
hardware quantities, seating dimensions and assembly order.

The report records 748 passing required checks, including 520 pairings across
13 outer-shell insertion positions. The assembly includes the inner thigh,
hip hub, both proximal pitch motors, stator plate, crank, sleeves, seated long
tie screws and nuts, and proposed motor-interface screws.

Fifteen driver paths remain blocked with the outer shell closed. They are
service-order limitations: fasten the knee stator and crank before fitting
the shell, and remove the shell to service those screws. The corrected lobed
inboard passage allows that staged assembly without adding fifteen roof holes.

Use three ordinary M3 × 60 cap screws and M3 nuts for the pod ties. Their heads
bear on the 5 mm metal sleeve ends at Y61; their nuts bear on the hub underside
at Y5. Nominal nut engagement is 2.4 mm with 1.6 mm screw projection. The three
retainer nuts bring the complete complex's M3 nut count to six.

Install the six M4 × 8 hip-output screws before the knee motor obstructs their
drivers. Use nine M3 × 8 socket-head screws for the knee stator and six M4 × 12
socket-head screws for its output crank. The existing hip and roll interfaces
retain their separate fastener types listed in the release report.

The long-tie holes and crank counterbores must be the corrected open versions
identified by the report's hashes. Older parts contain inaccessible screw-head
pockets. The original trapped stator recess also requires the corrected lobed
inboard opening; a few circular lobe holes do not clear its tangent webs.

The [RS02 manual](../../../proposals/sculpted/model/references/rs02-manual.pdf)
specifies nine M3 stator holes of 8 mm depth and six M4 output holes with a
7 mm minimum depth. All proposed motor screw penetrations stay below the
6 mm prototype limit. Confirm actual motor faces, hole depths and purchased
heads before tightening. The [drawing crop](rs02-side-datums.png) distinguishes
the 3.5 mm outer pilot projection from the separate 0.5 mm front step.

Optional printed tubes and bearing spacers check fit only. Square, deburr and
measure the metal compression tubes before using them to clamp the assembly.
No knee axle, 6001 bearings, pushrod or shoulder pins are needed at this stage.

The release covers nominal fit and a sampled assembly route. It does not certify
supplier inventory, printer tolerances, bearing preload, loaded strength or
native Fusion-derived manufacturing exports.
