# Native reinforced closure comparison

The native closure and both native motor cups match the reinforced screw-seat
proposal within the existing comparison gates. Each export is one valid solid.
The largest sampled boundary difference is 0.0000112 mm. Critical locating-boss,
pocket and screw-seat cylinder dimensions differ by less than 0.000000001 mm.
Exact export hashes and measurements are retained in
[native-closure-validation.json](native-closure-validation.json).

The actual closure clears both cups at all twenty-six sampled positions along
the axial removal path. Its extended rear lugs fit the 12.2 mm cup reliefs,
and its 9.9 mm locating pockets fit the 9.6 mm pilots. The nominal radial
clearances are 0.1 mm at the lugs and 0.15 mm at the pilots. Purchased parts,
printed fits and loaded deflection require separate checks.

The three recessed M4 × 16 button screws seat on complete supported floors.
Straight driver access clears the separated closure. The screws retain the
existing ordinary nuts; the three closure washers are removed. The reference
screw tops end at Y0, preserving the continuous 1 mm nominal axial clearance
to the rotating tie tips established by the separate hardware audit.

The existing hip and roll countersunk references need small placement changes
to bear on their actual conical seats. Shift the nine hip references by
−0.2 mm along Y. Shift the six front roll-output references by −0.2 mm along X;
reverse X for the rear assembly. Every checked shifted reference has zero solid
overlap and a positive conical contact footprint. Nominal thread penetration
is 5.1 mm at the hip and 4.2 mm at the roll motor. The new knee references
already have their own correct seats and require no such shift.

Thirty native section measurements across the front and rear cup-to-journal
connections retain at least 99.012% of the original area and 96.603% of the
original centroidal second moment of area. The smallest sampled area remains
657.836 mm². These measurements describe geometry, not qualified manufactured
strength, stiffness, preload, fatigue or polymer creep.

The complete corrected assembly still needs its broad static interference
screen before a replacement print package is issued. This report validates
the three exported custom parts, their sampled removal path and nominal screw
seating. It does not authorize powered or loaded operation.
