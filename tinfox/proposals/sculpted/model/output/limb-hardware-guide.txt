Structural assembly
There is no separate knee head or internal bearing-housing block. The two shin
halves contain the pushrod rocker, split bearing seats and axial retaining shoulders.
Their fore/aft split passes through the bearing axes. Opening the halves releases
the bearings radially; no blind pocket, drift slot or third internal housing is used.
The thigh remains two structural halves, with its motor face and service ring integral.
The hip-side shroud follows hip roll and is part of the motor carrier. The thigh
moves independently in pitch, so the shroud cannot bridge those two moving groups.

Printed carrier
Two parts form the printed carrier: a roll-side trunnion and a perpendicular hip
flange with integral shroud. A broad tongue/socket and two M4 button-head screws
join them. The hip flange is 5 mm thick. Print the two main mounting faces in
different bed orientations. This addresses layer direction but does not qualify
strength, clamp creep, temperature resistance or fatigue. Metal carriers remain
one piece. The separate motor-driven input crank is mechanically necessary.

Bench assembly and bearing service
1. Install the captive M4 nuts and compression sleeves into the open shin halves.
   Seat the M5 pivot nut in its side-loading pocket before closing the structural split.
2. Place both 6001 bearings and the inner-race spacer directly in the structural
   semicircular seats. Close the halves against their mating lands and install two
   M4 x 20 screws. The closure stops must preserve bore roundness and free rotation.
3. Fit the precision knee sleeve through the thigh and bearing inner races. Retain
   it with the recessed M8 through-bolt and outboard flange nut. Verify fit and clamp preload on the bench.
4. Install the paired flanged bushes and shoulder screws at the two pushrod pivots.
   Preserve their thrust faces and check free movement throughout the knee range.
5. To replace bearings, support the leg and remove the lower pushrod pivot screw.
   Release the axle retention and withdraw the precision sleeve. Remove the two
   M4 shin screws and open the structural halves. Lift out the bearings and spacer.
   Clean the exposed seats before reassembly; do not force bearings past a shoulder.

Fit and force precautions
The split housing releases bearings by opening, rather than pressing them axially
through blind stops. The shaft/bushing fits still require controlled assembly force.
Apply force to the race receiving an interference fit, never through bearing balls.
Do not use the clamp bolts to pull a misaligned bearing into its seat. Use printed
fit coupons and verify assembled roundness, axle alignment and free running.
For CNC, finish critical split bores with the halves fixed against the closure stops.

Prototype status
The printed set is for unloaded fit and hand-articulation checks. The knee linkage
can produce approximately 1.31 kN rod force at the 17 N m actuator peak reference.
Printed forks, motor carrier, clamp seats and bearing lands need physical load,
creep and fatigue testing. Solid infill alone does not establish strength.
Roll-bearing retainers/preload, the motor tie stack, exact screw engagements,
foot retention and wiring remain unfinished. No loaded gait is qualified.
