DIMENSIONAL FIT COUPON — 140 x 94 x 5 mm
Print flat, labels upward, in the same material/profile as the limb. No support is needed for the vertical holes and top-open pockets. The 34 mm series checks the metal shin-carrier OD, not a bare 28 mm bearing. Small circular holes check M3 clearance and 5 mm sleeve clearance. Hex pockets check M3 nut fit; AF means across flats. Measure after cooling; record actual hardware fits and tune clearance parameters before full-size printing. This is a dimensional coupon, not a strength test. Do not press bearings directly into plastic based on this result.
